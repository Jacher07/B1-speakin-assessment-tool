ELT Smart Evaluator

Speaking Assessment | CEFR B1 | Summative

Assessment Overview

Task: Talk about a memorable experience.

Level: B1 Intermediate

Duration: 2–3 minutes speaking + questions.

Objective: Students will communicate a past experience using connected ideas, appropriate vocabulary and generally accurate language.

Student Information

Student Instructions

  1. Think of a memorable experience.
  2. You have 2 minutes to prepare.
  3. Speak for 2–3 minutes.
  4. Explain what happened, when, where and how you felt.
  5. Answer two follow-up questions.

Follow-up questions:

  • What did you learn from this experience?
  • Would you like to experience it again? Why?
