B1 Speaking Assessment Tool

Summative Assessment · CEFR B1 · Oral Presentation

Assessment Task

Student task: Give a 3-minute oral presentation about a meaningful personal experience, a challenge you overcame, or an achievement. Explain what happened, how you felt, what you learned, and why the experience was important.

Instructions: The teacher listens and selects one performance level for each criterion.

Analytic Rubric

Choose one level per criterion. Each criterion is scored from 1 to 4. Maximum score: 20 points.
